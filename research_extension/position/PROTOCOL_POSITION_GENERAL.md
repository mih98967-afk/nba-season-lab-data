# Position and general opponent extension protocol — 17 November 2026

Status: research only; production adoption false. This protocol precedes new extension fitting and result inspection. Existing original model files and 16-Nov frozen forecasts remain immutable.

## Position identifiability
The source audit found historical NBA starter labels G/F/C/empty, with no exact PG/SG/SF/PF. A current Sports.ru-derived public catalog exists, but cannot establish historical position assignments or their publication vintage. We will not backfill current positions into past seasons. Five-position opponent testing and calendar promotion are blocked until independently valid as-of position evidence exists. We will implement a versioned mapping contract with exact identity matching, effective date, observation date and UNKNOWN/conflict handling; ambiguous identity and coarse NBA labels are not mappings.

## Incremental general opponent experiment
Use the original expanded-evidence feature frame and its selected Ridge matchup-context model recipe (the preserved context feature set, excluding the separately tested injury additions), not a fresh model architecture. All data are historical 2023/24–2025/26; no 2026/27 outcome will be read or used by this experiment. Ex-ante membership and >=5 prior appearances define the original eligible cohort.

Generate strictly pregame residuals from expanding monthly Ridge forecasts: each monthly fit uses only earlier target dates, and minimum training support is 1000 played records. Before that threshold, omit residual updates (never substitute in-sample predictions). Ridge uses the preserved selected context feature set including expected minutes, player quality and as-of tempo/context, with fixed alpha 500. Residuals are actual FP minus that pregame expectation. Actual minutes and actual game pace are not input at issue time; uncertainty in minutes remains part of the residual. This is a predictive opponent adjustment, not a causal defensive rating.

At each target date, summarize only residuals from strictly earlier dates by opponent. We retain season history with fixed 60-calendar-day half-life; no current or same-day outcomes enter features. Effective weighted count, mean and standard-error diagnostics are stored; prior shrinkage pulls adjustment to zero. Fixed prior strengths 50 and 150 weighted player-games are the only candidates. No search over half-lives, clipping limits, opponent identities, or exposed audit results.

Matched ablations: original Ridge context; same Ridge plus the single generic opponent residual mean feature (one candidate per prior strength). Support/dispersion are diagnostics, not additional predictors. No extra player/club identity, current-game role or outcome feature. Selection uses original chronological boundaries: train 2023/24; select 2024/25 through 31 December 2024; refit selected recipe on train+select; intercept correction on 1–23 January 2025; interval block 24 January–15 February is unused for this mean-only extension; confirmation from 16 February 2025; 2025/26 is explicitly an already exposed historical audit. Before fitting, selection is refined to two expanding chronological development folds: train season 2023/24 and validate October–November 2024; then train through November 2024 and validate December 2024. Selection uses pooled squared error / RMSE across both folds; reject changed recipe if it does not improve pooled selection RMSE. This matches the parallel recency protocol. Report MAE, RMSE, bias, date/player-cluster paired uncertainty and calibration by opponent.

Both baseline and selected extension are refitted/calibrated identically. New features must additionally improve confirmation and exposed audit to be retained as a shadow candidate; no result promotes a five-position calendar or optimizer. This experiment does not change P(play) or predictive intervals. Conditional scores alone do not prove unconditional, economic, or deployment gains.

## Required tests
Position snapshots cannot apply before observed/effective time or to other seasons; ambiguous mappings stay UNKNOWN; current starter role is rejected; conflicting positions fail closed. Opponent summaries exclude same-day/future outcomes, decay and shrink correctly, and remain finite at cold start. Altering a future outcome must not change earlier features.
